# Module 3: the Master CV

Goal: one strong base CV, built from the confirmed Fact Bank, in a structure that works with applicant tracking systems (ATS). The person confirms the result. A Master CV fits almost any job ad without changes; adjusting it to the ad (module 4) raises the chances further. Tell the person both things.

## Ways to start
- **From the Fact Bank** (recommended): every line comes from a confirmed fact.
- **From the current CV**: keep the person's wording where it is true, check every line against the Bank, list the differences.
- **Both**: the current CV gives the shape, the Bank fills the gaps.
If the person searches for different kinds of roles, build one Master CV per role family.

## Structure (fixed)
1. **Header**: line 1 = name and a job title; line 2 = location, work mode (for example "Open to remote") and contacts. Take name and contacts from the Contact block of the Bank. Nothing else.
2. **PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY**: three short paragraphs at most, facts only. Each line cites the IDs of the facts it restates.
3. **CORE COMPETENCIES**: keywords separated by `|`, then a `Tools:` line. Reuse words from the text of confirmed facts; do not coin new skills. A tool with `In production: no` or with no role goes in only with its true label (for example "Kubernetes (training course and side project)") or is left out.
4. **WORK EXPERIENCE**: for each role: title, employer and type, location, dates, then bullets that carry a number or a clear outcome.
5. **KEY ACHIEVEMENTS**: optional. Include it only to bring up results that do not fit the experience bullets; at most three bullets.
6. **EDUCATION**
7. **CERTIFICATIONS**: only those held; "in progress" only with a real expected date.
8. **LANGUAGES** with level
9. **WORK AUTHORISATION**: only if the person wants it; it answers the recruiter's unspoken question.

## Formatting rules (ATS)
Single column. No tables, text boxes, columns or sidebars. No headers or footers (many parsers drop them, and contacts live there in many templates). No photo, icons, logos or charts. Calibri, Arial or Georgia, 10 to 12 pt. Section headings exactly as above. Dates as `MM/YYYY – MM/YYYY` with an en dash; a current role is `MM/YYYY – Present` and always has a start date. Bullets are `•` or a real list. Submit `.docx` unless the form says PDF. **At most 2 pages, which in a text chat means up to about 900 words.**

## Two versions
- **Working version**: every bullet ends with its fact tag, for example `[F012]`. This is what the checks use. The header comes from the Contact block and needs no tag.
- **Clean version**: the same text without tags, ready to send.

## Checks before you show it
Every line except the header matches a confirmed fact; competency keywords reuse words from facts; dates use one format; one column, no tables, no photo; up to about 900 words; the header has exactly two lines. Report each check. For a failed check, show the exact line.

## Confirm
Show the working version and the check results. Ask for changes. Save the Master CV when the person confirms. Then tell them it is ready for most jobs as it is, and that tailoring it to a specific ad still raises the chances.
